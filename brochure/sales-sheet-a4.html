What it covers

The outreach

Camps, recalls, price lists, festival greetings. Upload a list, send it one patient at a time on a human interval, and stop it mid-run from the same screen.

The conversation

Enquiries answered from your own documents, at your own pace, with a follow-up scheduled days out — and a switch that hands any single chat back to a person.

The record

Every photo, PDF and lab sheet you send yourself is read, attached to the right patient, and grouped so you can pull a whole category at once.

How it works — and what holds it back
It sends the way a person sends.

One message, a pause, the next. You set the pace; you can pause, resume or stop the run while it is in flight.

Default gap 25 s, randomised each time. The randomiser can never pull the gap below 30 s or 15% of your setting — whichever is larger. A mis-set jitter cannot become a burst.
Every message is written before it is sent.

The assistant drafts a pool of rewordings so 400 people do not receive one identical block of text. Nothing leaves until you pick the wording.

The pool refreshes every 100 contacts. If drafting fails, it falls back to your original message — never to silence, never to a blank.
The number stays linked.

Pairing lives in your database, not in a folder on a server. Updates, restarts and moves do not cost you a QR scan.

Each disconnect reason has its own retry ladder — 500 ms for a routine restart, widening to 10 minutes for a hard logout — with up to 3 s of random jitter so a fleet of numbers never reconnects in lockstep.
Nobody gets asked the same thing twice.

Three quick lines from a patient are read as one question and answered once, not three times.

A 5-second settle window joins them. Photos queue behind each other per contact, so a caption sent after four images is still applied to the right case.
Documents are filed, not dumped.

Send several images for one patient as a single case, close it with one word, and the whole set is read together as one visit.

Each file is stored before it is read, and the stored copy is deleted again if the record fails to write. There is no half-saved case.
Retrieval by category, not by scrolling.

The reader assigns a three-level subject index — region, procedure, stage — so you can ask for a whole group at once.

The moment you correct an index it is marked as yours and re-runs will not overwrite it.
Today's position

What you are losing today

  • The knee film you sent yourself at 11 p.m. — now four hundred photographs down your gallery, somewhere between a screenshot and a lunch.
  • The Sunday enquiry about a package price, answered Tuesday, by which time they had already booked down the road.
  • The Friday evening a staff member spent pasting the same paragraph into a hundred and eighty chats, changing one name at a time.
  • The patient who wrote STOP three campaigns ago and is still on the list, because the list lives in a spreadsheet that never heard them.
  • The Monday morning you discovered the number had been logged out since Friday, and nobody knew which messages never went.
What the system refuses to do

It will not message anyone who typed STOP. They are added to the block list and confirmed automatically; every later run records them as skipped, with the reason.

It will not read clinical documents from a stranger. Only the connected number's own messages are accepted — and if it cannot confirm the sender, it refuses rather than guesses.

It will not send a campaign with a missing attachment. The missing filename is shown on the campaign before you press send, not discovered on contact forty.

It will not repeat a follow-up. The same text to the same person inside 24 hours is cancelled and logged as a duplicate.

It will not fire a bot-written line you have not chosen. Scheduling is blocked until a wording is selected.

It will not burn a message on a dead line. It holds, retries, and after three days tells you plainly that it gave up.
